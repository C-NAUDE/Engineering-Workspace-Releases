SYMBOLIC INTEGRATION

See what the integrator can do.

The offline C++ engine works with polynomials, rational functions, trigonometric and hyperbolic expressions, exponentials, logarithms, and many algebraic radicals. It transforms expressions and checks candidate antiderivatives by differentiating them. Results can include domain conditions, and the app distinguishes completed, unsupported, and verification-limited outcomes.

01

Use the calculus screen for one expression or the batch workspace for a list of commands.

02

Inspect the result

ABOUT

Powerful mathematics should be accessible.

Engineering Workspace is a free application for students and anyone who wants to explore mathematics and engineering calculations. It is currently in beta development. Some difficult integrals remain unsupported, and a reported result should be checked in context before use.
